info About Apex Arena

Apex Badminton & Multi-Sport Arena is Austin's premier indoor racquet destination, equipped with 8 Olympic-grade badminton courts configured on shock-absorbing Taraflex multi-layer synthetic flooring. Designed for serious club competitors, recreational leagues, and casual match play alike, the complex features uniform 800-lux vertical anti-glare LED illumination, high clearance 32-foot ceilings, and full acoustic baffle suppression.

QuickCourt players enjoy automated zero-wait turnstile check-in via PIN code, frictionless digital lighting triggers synced to your reservation block, and a fully appointed pro shop with on-site high-tension electronic stringing technicians.
